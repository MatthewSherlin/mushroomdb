### Added
- **`stDev`, `stDevP`, `percentileCont` and `percentileDisc` aggregates.** `stDev(x)` is the sample standard deviation (null with fewer than two values) and `stDevP(x)` the population one; `percentileCont(x, p)` interpolates and `percentileDisc(x, p)` takes the nearest rank, with `p` a number from 0 to 1 given as a literal or a parameter. Nulls are skipped, a non-numeric value is a type error, and all four work with grouping, `DISTINCT` and aggregate `WITH`. (QL-04)
