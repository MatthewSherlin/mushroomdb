### Added
- **Bolt `RUN`, `PULL` and `DISCARD`.** A connection runs a statement and streams its rows in pieces (`PULL n`, `n = -1` for all) or drops them. Outside a transaction a read uses the lock-free reader and a write is one durable commit, with the same role handling as the HTTP `/query` route; inside one the statement runs in the transaction and sees its own writes, and several results can stay open at once, pulled by `qid`. A null parameter is left out, as on the HTTP route. An idle open result is closed after the same idle limit as a transaction. Bookmarks (`mushroomdb:commit:<n>`) make a statement wait for the store to reach them. A row holding a value over 4 GiB or a structure with more than 65535 fields fails the `PULL` with a clear `FAILURE` instead of being truncated. (TX-11).
