### Added
- **Bolt in the deploy templates, safely by default**: the Fly template serves Bolt on 7687 behind Fly's `tls` handler, the Compose template maps it to `127.0.0.1` only, and the Kubernetes manifest adds a ClusterIP port. Render has no Bolt (a web service is HTTP-only) and Railway needs its TCP proxy enabled by hand; both templates say so. `MUSHROOMDB_BOLT_PORT` and the advertised host are set where Bolt is on.
