### Added
- **Dates, times, durations and bytes travel over Bolt.** Date, local date-time, date-time with a UTC offset, duration and binary values go out in results and come in as `RUN` parameters, at both Bolt 5.0 and 4.4, each version using its own date-time structure. A value sent, stored and read back arrives unchanged. A date-time parameter with sub-microsecond nanoseconds is refused rather than rounded. Named time zones, Time, LocalTime and points are refused with an error that names the type. (TX-20)
