### Added
- **Cypher `UNWIND` over any list expression.** `UNWIND range(1, 5) AS i`, `UNWIND $items AS x`, and `UNWIND tail(n.tags) AS x` now parse and run, not just a literal list, a property or a bare variable. `null` and an empty list yield no rows; a `null` element is a row bound to null; a non-list value is an error. (QL-09).
