- **Arrow results carry the new types natively.** A column of all `Date` is `Date32`, `LocalDateTime` is `Timestamp(µs)`, `DateTime` is `Timestamp(µs, "+00:00")`, `Duration` is `Duration(µs)` and `Binary` is `LargeBinary`. `DateTime` keeps the UTC instant but drops each value's offset, and `Duration` drops months and sub-microsecond nanos; a column mixing types stays Utf8 text.
