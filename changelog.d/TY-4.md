- **Arrow results carry the new types natively.** A column of all `Date` is `Date32`, `LocalDateTime` is `Timestamp(µs)`, `DateTime` is `Timestamp(µs, "+00:00")`, `Duration` is `Duration(µs)` and `Binary` is `LargeBinary`. `DateTime` keeps the UTC instant but drops each value's offset; `Duration` truncates sub-microsecond nanos, and a Duration column with any non-zero month is Utf8 ISO 8601 text; a column mixing types stays Utf8 text.
