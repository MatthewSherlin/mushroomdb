- **`mushroomdb import` bulk-loads a nodes CSV and a relationships CSV** with the usual bulk-import headers (`:ID`, `:LABEL`, `:START_ID`, `:END_ID`, `:TYPE`, typed columns such as `age:int` and `tags:string[]`) (PF-03a). Batches of 50,000 commit one WAL frame each, one snapshot ends the import, and a bad row stops it at its file and line (`--ignore-errors` skips it). One label per node: `Person;Employee` is refused row by row. Relationship property columns are refused: edges carry no properties.
- **A large `ingest` or `write_batch` no longer slows quadratically with its size.** Validating each node insert walked every entry the batch had already recorded, so a 50,000-node batch spent over 90% of its time there; 100,000 nodes in 50,000-row batches took 41 s and now take under 1 s. Results are unchanged (PF-03a).
- **`mushroomdb snapshot` of a store that has never committed now succeeds.** It failed with "No such file or directory" and left `snapshot.intent` behind (ledger row 119). `mushroomdb import` refuses `:ID(Space)` / `:START_ID(Space)` headers (id spaces are not supported in 0.10), refuses a multi-label cell whatever the array delimiter, and in strict mode commits no part of a relationship batch the engine rejects.
