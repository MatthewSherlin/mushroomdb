### Added
- **Temporal map constructors in full, and the namespaced temporal functions.** `date`, `localdatetime` and `datetime` maps take every openCypher field for their type: ISO week dates (`{year: 1817, week: 1, dayOfWeek: 2}`), quarters (`quarter`, `dayOfQuarter`), `ordinalDay`, the sub-second fields as parts of one fraction, a `timezone` offset for `datetime`, `epochSeconds`/`epochMillis`, and a base value with overrides (`date({date: d, day: 1})`, `datetime({datetime: x, timezone: '+05:00'})`); `duration` maps take fractions in any field. New functions: `date.truncate`, `localdatetime.truncate` and `datetime.truncate(unit, value[, fields])`; `date.statement()`, `.transaction()` and `.realtime()` on `date`, `localdatetime` and `datetime` (the current instant, optionally on an offset's clock); `datetime.fromepoch(s, ns)` and `datetime.fromepochmillis(ms)`; `duration.between`, `duration.inMonths`, `duration.inDays` and `duration.inSeconds`. A temporal constructor or function now gives a value or an error naming the function, never null for a bad input: an unreadable string, an unknown, conflicting or out-of-range field, or a wrong argument type is an error (it was null); a null argument or null field is still null. A `datetime` string's offset may omit its colon (`+0100`). Named time zones and the `time`/`localtime` namespaces are refused by name. The openCypher TCK pass rate rises from 38.8% to 52.8% (temporal scenarios: 45 to 504 passed). (QL-TF)
