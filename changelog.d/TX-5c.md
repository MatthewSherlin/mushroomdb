### Changed
- **A commit no reader is waiting for no longer pays for publishing.** `SharedDb` still publishes every commit's snapshot as the write lock is released, so read-your-write and the lock-free capture are unchanged. It now wakes the background builder for that snapshot only when a reader has captured since the previous commit. Otherwise the builder builds the latest version on its own 2 ms tick, and sleeps once 100 ms pass with nothing to build; a version replaced before anyone read it is dropped as it is replaced. A write scope also reads the WAL's length and file identity with one `stat` instead of two. A plain single-node commit with readers paused (the concurrency gate's writer probe, fsync off, 10k nodes, median of 9 interleaved runs) costs 0.99× v0.9.2's, down from 1.19× on the TX-7 base; with no reader threads at all it costs 1.01× (base: 1.16×). With 8 readers running it is unchanged. Numbers are provisional (loaded machine). The concurrency gate's paused-readers check is back to a single measurement at ≤ 1.15× v0.9.2 (the 1.05× bar plus noise). (TX-5c)
