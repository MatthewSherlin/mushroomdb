### Added
- **The catalog procedures `db.labels()`, `db.relationshipTypes()`, `db.propertyKeys()`, `db.indexes()`, `db.constraints()`, `dbms.components()` and `db.schema.visualization()` run under `CALL`, and list only what the caller can see.** A role, a mask or a namespace never learns a label, relationship type, property key, index or constraint that only nodes outside its scope carry, on `query_masked`, a role-scoped write, the lock-free reader, HTTP `/query`, Bolt `RUN` and the MCP `query` tool. `db.indexes()` and `db.constraints()` return the rows `SHOW INDEXES` / `SHOW CONSTRAINTS` return, with `labelsOrTypes` and `properties` as lists; `dbms.components()` reports `mushroomdb`, the engine version and the edition `open`.
