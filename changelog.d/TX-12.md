### Added
- **Bolt `BEGIN`, `COMMIT` and `ROLLBACK`, and causal bookmarks.** A transaction reads a snapshot frozen at `BEGIN`, sees its own writes and commits as one atomic batch; `COMMIT` replies with a `mushroomdb:commit:<n>` bookmark, and a conflict is a transient failure the driver retries. `BEGIN` takes `bookmarks` (it waits for the store to reach them), `db` (checked like `ROUTE`'s) and `mode` (`"r"` refuses writes). A `RESET` sent while a bookmark wait is running ends the wait at once; one sent during a running statement takes effect when it returns. (TX-12)

### Changed
- **Bolt null parameters are null.** A null parameter now binds `Value::Null`, so `$p IS NULL` is true for it; before it was left out and the statement failed with a missing parameter. (TX-12)
