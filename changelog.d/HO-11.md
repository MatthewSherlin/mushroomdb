- **Graceful shutdown flushes the backup and releases the lease**: on SIGTERM or SIGINT, `serve` drains in-flight requests (up to 25 s, plain HTTP and TLS alike), takes the shutdown snapshot on a blocking thread (skipped if it overruns; the WAL is durable), then tells the backup worker to stop. The worker uploads the last WAL segment and snapshot, writes a final restore point, and deletes the bucket lease, so a successor holds it within seconds instead of waiting out the TTL (about 40 s). The whole sequence finishes within the new `--shutdown-timeout-secs` (default 55: 25 drain, 8 snapshot, 22 backup). A second SIGTERM or SIGINT exits at once. The phases scale down proportionally for a shorter timeout, and the backup's own bounds shrink to fit. The Fly, Compose and Railway templates allow 60 s; Render's shutdown delay is a fixed 30 s with a disk, so its template runs `serve --shutdown-timeout-secs 25`. `/ready` never follows backup health
