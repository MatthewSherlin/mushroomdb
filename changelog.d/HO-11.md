- **Graceful shutdown flushes the backup and releases the lease**: on SIGTERM or SIGINT, once in-flight requests have drained and the shutdown snapshot is taken, `serve` tells the backup worker to stop. The worker uploads the last WAL segment and snapshot, writes a final restore point, and deletes the bucket lease, so a successor holds it within seconds instead of waiting out the TTL (about 40 s). The wait is bounded by `MUSHROOMDB_BACKUP_SHUTDOWN_GRACE_MS` plus `MUSHROOMDB_BACKUP_OP_TIMEOUT_MS` plus 2 s (22 s at the defaults); the Fly and Docker Compose templates now allow 60 s. `/ready` never follows backup health
