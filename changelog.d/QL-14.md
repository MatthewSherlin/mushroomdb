### Added
- **Cypher `reduce(acc = init, x IN list | expr)`.** Folds a list into one value; works wherever an expression does (`WHERE`, `RETURN`, `ORDER BY`, `UNWIND`, a `SET` value, a function argument) and nests with itself and with list comprehensions. `acc` and `x` are visible only inside `expr` and shadow outer names there. An empty list gives `init`; a null list gives null. `reduce()` in the `RETURN` of a write statement is refused by name before anything is written. `+` now also joins two strings or two lists (it was a type error).
