### Changed
- **The first write after a reader fold no longer copies the store under the write lock.** The column store, the equality and range index and the key map are now copy-on-write in pieces, so the commit after a fold copies only what it writes. First commit after a fold, fsync off, median: about 151 → 0.46 ms (indexed `name`) and 41 → 0.27 ms (plain field) at 500k nodes; 465 → 0.42 ms and 140 → 0.31 ms at 1.4M nodes. The worst 10k-row bulk commit at 1M nodes falls from 738 to 104 ms; its median rises from 51 to 77 ms, still 0.42× v0.9.2. Numbers are provisional (loaded machine). Ledger row 126. In `mushroomdb-storage`: a new public `cow` module (`CowVec`, `CowMap`, `IdSet`, `HashIndex`); `UnifiedIndex::postings` now returns `&CowMap<ValueKey, IdSet>` and `UnifiedIndex::lookup_eq` returns `&IdSet`. (TX-7)
- **A batch that creates edges and deletes or renames nodes commits in linear time.** It used to filter every pending edge once per deleted or renamed node: 20k rows took 586–666 ms, and now take 179–201 ms. Ledger row 130. (TX-7)

### Added
- **Concurrency gate.** CI's new `concurrency-gate` job checks that, on a 10k-node store with a writer committing 50 times a second, 16 concurrent neighbourhood reads keep p50 ≤ 0.5 ms and p99 ≤ 2 ms. It also checks that a plain commit under concurrent readers stays within 2× its cost with no readers, while those readers complete at least 10k reads/s. The same scenario is the Criterion bench `readers_p50_p99_under_sustained_writes`. (TX-7)
