### Added
- **`mushroomdb serve --bolt-port <port>` serves Bolt beside HTTP** (or `MUSHROOMDB_BOLT_PORT`; off when unset). The listener binds `--addr`'s IP, accepts exactly the credentials HTTP accepts (the token, role tokens and `keys.json` keys with their tiers, so a `read` key cannot write over Bolt), and is refused on a non-loopback address with no authentication, as HTTP is. With `--tls-cert`/`--tls-key` it serves the same certificate (HTTP and Bolt build one TLS config) and is TLS unless `--bolt-plaintext` (`MUSHROOMDB_BOLT_TLS=off`) is given; `--bolt-tls` without a certificate is refused. `ROUTE` advertises `--bolt-advertised-host` and `--bolt-advertised-port` (`MUSHROOMDB_BOLT_ADVERTISED_HOST`/`_PORT`); a wildcard `--addr` with no advertised host is refused at startup. Also `--bolt-database`, `--bolt-max-connections` (default 256, `MUSHROOMDB_BOLT_MAX_CONNECTIONS`), `--bolt-tx-timeout-secs` (default 30) and `--bookmark-timeout-secs` (default 5). A client that does not finish the handshake and `HELLO` in 10 seconds is closed; a client over the connection limit gets a transient failure and is closed; a panic in one connection ends only that connection. On SIGTERM the listener stops accepting with HTTP at the start of the drain, closes each connection between requests (rolling back an open transaction), and cuts off what is left at the drain bound.
### Changed
- **Bolt database names are compared without regard to case**, in `ROUTE`, `RUN` and `BEGIN` alike.
- **Bolt logs the detail of a storage failure, a busy store or corruption on the server** at the point where the client's message replaces it with fixed text.
